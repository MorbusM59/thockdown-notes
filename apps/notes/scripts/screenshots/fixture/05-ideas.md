# Small Ideas

A scratchpad for things that are not yet anything.

- A map of every bakery within walking distance, ranked by their rye bread
- Learn to identify five birds by their song alone
- A shelf for postcards from friends, swapped out each season
- Write one letter a month, on paper
- Plant a tree somewhere it will still be standing in fifty years

## Half-formed

What would a weekend look like with no screens at all? Probably a long walk, a long lunch, and a book finished in one sitting.
