# Balcony Garden Plan

Three planters, one railing box, and a lot of afternoon sun.

## Spring

- Sweet peas along the railing, sown in March
- Radishes and spring onions in the long planter
- Nasturtiums to trail over the edge

## Summer

- Two cherry tomato plants, staked early
- Basil, thyme and a pot of mint kept on its own
- Water in the evening, every day once it is warm

## Watering cheat sheet

| Plant | How often | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tomatoes | Daily | Deep, at the base |
| Herbs | Every other day | Let the top dry out |
| Sweet peas | Twice a week | More once flowering |
