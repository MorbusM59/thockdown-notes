# Weekend Sourdough

A slow loaf for a slow Saturday. Start the levain on Friday evening and the bread is out of the oven by Saturday lunch.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Baker's % |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Bread flour | 450 g | 90% |
| Whole wheat flour | 50 g | 10% |
| Water | 375 g | 75% |
| Levain | 100 g | 20% |
| Salt | 10 g | 2% |

## Method

1. **Autolyse** the flours and 350 g of the water for an hour.
2. Add the levain, then the salt with the remaining water.
3. Four sets of *stretch and folds*, thirty minutes apart.
4. Bulk ferment until the dough has grown by about half.
5. Shape, then proof overnight in the fridge.
6. Bake at 250 °C in a covered pot for 20 minutes, then uncovered for 25.

> The dough is ready when it jiggles like a waterbed and a floured finger leaves a dent that springs back slowly.
