# Community Garden Meeting

**Attendees:** the planting group, the tool shed volunteers, and two new neighbours.

## Agenda

1. Spring planting schedule
2. Repairs to the tool shed roof
3. A shared compost rota

## Decisions

- The planting day moves to the first Saturday in April.
- The roof gets fixed before the rains, materials paid from the garden fund.
- Compost is turned on Sundays, in pairs, on a rota pinned to the shed door.
