# Ridge Trail, Day Two

Left the hut at first light. The valley was still full of cloud, and for the first hour the ridge felt like a narrow island above a white sea.

## Route

- **Distance:** 14.2 km
- **Ascent:** 920 m
- **Time:** 6 h 40 min, with a long lunch at the saddle

The marked path drops off the crest twice to skirt rock towers. The second detour is the steeper one: loose scree, then a short chain section that is easier than it looks.

## Things worth remembering

1. Water at the spring below the saddle, nowhere else after it.
2. The last bus from the village leaves at 17:20.
3. Bilberries everywhere along the descent in late summer.

> Walk the ridge east to west in the morning and the sun stays at your back the whole way.
